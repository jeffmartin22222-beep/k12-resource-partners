ABOUT K12 RESOURCE PARTNERS

K-12 Experience. Better Connections.

K12 Resource Partners connects schools with education companies offering technology, curriculum, services, and other solutions designed for K-12 education.

Built With an Understanding of Schools

K12 Resource Partners was created with a simple idea: the best education partnerships start with understanding the schools they are meant to serve.

Our background in K-12 education, school leadership, educational technology, and the education solutions market gives us a practical understanding of how schools evaluate products, make decisions, and build partnerships.

We use that experience to connect education providers with schools where their solutions can make a meaningful impact.

What we bring

  • Practical understanding of school priorities and decision-making
  • Experience across K-12 education and education technology
  • Relationships grounded in trust, responsiveness, and fit
